# Code of conduct

Hobnix is a small project that people run in their homes. We want everyone who files an issue, reads the docs or sends a pull request to be
treated with respect, whatever their experience, background or the hardware they own. This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/, CC BY 4.0).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux, NixOS or servers.
- Criticise the code, the design or the docs, not the person. Give a reason and, if you can, a way forward.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, or later, to a change. Say what you need; do not demand.
- Assume good faith. When something reads as rude, ask what was meant before you answer in kind.
- Keep other people's private information private: addresses, hostnames, logs with names in them. Do not post it, and do not ask for it
  in public.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or sexualised language and attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would call unprofessional in a project that asks for help and gives it.

## Where it applies

In this organisation's repositories (issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, commit messages) and wherever someone speaks for the
project in public.

## Reporting and what happens

Report a problem **privately** to the maintainers at **<CONTACT-EMAIL-TO-BE-SET-BY-OWNER>**. A report about a maintainer goes to another
maintainer; say so in the message. We read every report, keep the reporter's name private, and answer within a week.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, close an issue or pull request, or block a person for a while or for good. Most
cases end with a short, friendly note asking the person to change how they write. A security problem is not a conduct problem: see
`SECURITY.md`.
